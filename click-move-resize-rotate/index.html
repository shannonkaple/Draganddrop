Made for elementary Chromebook practice. Stars save separately for each teacher and student number on this browser. No accounts, no ads.

How to Play 🎮

  1. SELECT: Click the middle of an item. A purple box and little square handles will appear.
  2. MOVE: Use your keyboard arrow keys, or click and drag the middle of the item.
  3. RESIZE: Drag a corner handle to make the selected item bigger or smaller.
  4. ROTATE: Click an object, then drag the round curved-arrow handle above it to tilt it like in Google Slides.
  5. MATCH: Make your item fit the dotted outline. Items turn green when placed correctly.

For students: no typing. This game uses mouse/trackpad and keyboard arrows, not touch controls.
