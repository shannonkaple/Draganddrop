# Map Two — eight-world artwork handoff (November 5, 2026)

**Map Two is a fresh map after all 64 original Map One missions.** It is never a row of levels below the old planet maps. Students must complete the entire eight-planet Map One before the Map Two navigation button appears.

## Format and visual contract
- One **original illustrated Map Two master map**: `arrow-naut/assets/map-two/lost-worlds-map.jpg`, **1672 × 941**, landscape 16:9, same visual format/proportions as the existing original eight-planet map, not the same content.
- Eight distinct themed areas/islands connected by an original winding path. No reuse of Map One's original planets or locations.
- Leave room for actual HTML level-button overlays; labels/locks must remain selectable and readable on a 1366 × 768 Chromebook.
- Each world's individual illustrated game/map backdrop is a **separate 1672 × 941 JPEG**; each rescued friend is an individual transparent PNG. **Never crop an atlas or substitute an emoji**. Use object-fit:contain.
- Style: cohesive colorful polished cartoon illustrations, inviting to ages 6–8. Not photorealistic. Maintain approved characters and avoid remaking already approved assets.

## Gameplay rules
- Map One's original 64 levels, original star balances, friend rescues and saved student keys remain untouched.
- Only after 64/64 original missions are complete does the separate Map Two entrance appear.
- Map Two presents its own eight-theme illustrated map, not the old planet map or a bottom row on its old world screens.
- Each new world should have eight genuinely varied missions; a rescue at the eighth mission. Mixed selecting, moving with arrows, mouse/trackpad dragging, resizing for second grade; moving bad guys on appropriate levels.
- Do not expose incomplete worlds as playable. Publish small complete batches and test the Chrome browser behavior.
